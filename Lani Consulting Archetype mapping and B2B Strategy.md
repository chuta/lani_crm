# **5\. The Commercial Trigger Map**

When evaluating any organisation, do not ask only:

> “Can LANI sell something to them?”

Ask:

> **“What is changing inside this organisation that could create a need for LANI?”**

Look for these triggers:

| Trigger | Likely opportunity |
| ----- | ----- |
| New CEO or leadership team | Strategy and transformation |
| Expansion into a new market | Market-entry advisory |
| New regulation | Compliance, training and process review |
| New funding or grant | Programme implementation and M\&E |
| Merger or restructuring | Process and organisational advisory |
| New technology investment | Digital transformation |
| Rapid recruitment | Human capital development |
| Operational inefficiencies | Process optimisation |
| Sustainability commitments | Sustainability advisory |
| New public-sector programme | Implementation consortium |
| New product or service | Research, strategy and market positioning |

This converts B2B partnership development from random outreach into **trigger-based opportunity discovery**.

# **6\. How to Prioritise Your Opportunity Map**

I recommend a simple five-dimensional qualification system.

Score each prospect from **1 to 5**, but use the score for internal prioritisation rather than presenting an overall ranking as an objective truth.

| Dimension | Question |
| ----- | ----- |
| Strategic fit | Does the organisation need a capability LANI can credibly deliver? |
| Access | Do we have a realistic route to the decision-maker? |
| Commercial potential | Is there a meaningful budget or funded programme? |
| Urgency | Is there a current trigger or defined business problem? |
| Conversion pathway | Can we identify the next step toward a proposal or engagement? |

# **7\. Your 90-Day Target Market Structure**

For your first three months, I would organise the pipeline into **four commercial lanes**.

### **Lane 1: Immediate Revenue**

Existing relationships and organisations with identifiable needs that could convert within 30–90 days.

### **Lane 2: Strategic Accounts**

Large institutions where the sales cycle may be longer but the potential engagement is substantial.

### **Lane 3: Partnership Channels**

Organisations capable of generating multiple referrals, consortium opportunities or recurring engagements.

### **Lane 4: Emerging Markets**

Fintech, digital assets, AI, technology infrastructure and other emerging sectors where LANI can develop new offerings.

This structure prevents you from spending all your time on long-term relationship building while neglecting near-term revenue opportunities.
